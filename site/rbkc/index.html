Net share

Net affordable dwellings divided by all net dwellings. Losses can make this volatile or impossible to interpret.

Positive completions only

Affordable gains divided by all positive completion records. This removes the denominator effect caused by losses.

Multi-year delivery compared with the 35% benchmark

Longer periods reduce the effect of individual schemes and annual losses.

Flagged years

Loss effect: the two measures differ by at least 15 percentage points, or net delivery is non-positive. Major scheme: one address supplies at least 50 affordable dwellings and at least half of the year's positive affordable delivery. Address groups are not necessarily planning applications.

Tenure split target (by dwelling)

All figures count dwellings rather than habitable rooms and are indicative. Annual net shares are omitted where total net delivery is non-positive. Policy benchmark: 60% social rent and 40% intermediate within affordable delivery. This dataset groups social and intermediate tenure together as “Affordable”, so it cannot calculate or chart either tenure share. The split target is shown here for context only.
